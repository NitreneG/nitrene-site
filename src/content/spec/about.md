# About NitreneG

An undergraduate student in the [Department of Physics](https://phy.sustech.edu.cn), [SUSTech](https://www.sustech.edu.cn).

My research interests center on:

- Theoretical and Computational Condensed Matter Physics
- Symmetry theory 
- Scientific computing.

## Research

I am particularly fascinated by problems in which abstract mathematical structures lead to concrete constraints on systems. In Condensed Matter Physics, I am especially interested in symmetry-based approaches to electronic structure and magnetism, including 

- **Spin Group** theory 
- **Unconventional Magnetism** 
- **Spintronics**.

One of my current projects, `SSG4Wann`, a Python package for constructing symmetrized Wannier
Hamiltonians using Oriented Spin Space Groups, has already been released on [GitHub](https://github.com/LiuQH-lab/SSG4Wann) and [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/SSG4Wann/).


## Tools & Computing

Most of my current work is done with `Python` and the electronic-structure ecosystem around `VASP` and `Wannier90`. I work with numerical linear algebra, group representations and symmetry operations.
