## 友链交换

欢迎交换友链~

### 申请条件

1. 网站可以正常访问，并有一定数量的原创内容、项目记录、知识分享或其他实质性内容。
2. 不要求长期或频繁更新，但网站不应只有空白页面、默认模板或大量无实际内容的占位页面。
3. 网站内容与运营方式应当合法合规，不包含恶意软件、欺诈、违法或其他可能伤害访问者的内容。
4. 申请前，请先将 **NitreneGlog** 添加到你的友链页面，并确保该页面可以公开访问。
5. 请通过邮件 [nitreneg@gmail.com](mailto:nitreneg@gmail.com)，或在 [Guestbook](/guestbook/) 的评论区留下申请。
6. 申请时请提供网站名称、网站地址、头像或 Logo 地址、网站简介，以及方便联系你的方式。


### 维护说明

- 不定期检查无法访问、长期失效或内容明显异常的链接可能会被移除。
- 如果网站迁移、更换域名、头像或简介，请及时与我更新信息。
- 如果本站友链被移除，我也可能移除对应的友链。
- 因暂时故障而被移除的链接，在网站恢复后可以再次联系我添加。

## Link Exchange

Link exchange requests are welcome!!!

### Requirements

1. Your website should be publicly accessible and contain original writing, project records, knowledge sharing, or other meaningful content.
2. Frequent or long-term updates are not required, but the site should not consist only of blank pages, an untouched template, or placeholder content.
3. The website and its content must be legal and should not contain malware, scams, illegal material, or anything that may harm visitors.
4. Before applying, please add **NitreneGlog** to your own links page and make sure that page is publicly accessible.
5. Send your request by email at [nitreneg@gmail.com](mailto:nitreneg@gmail.com), or leave a comment in the [Guestbook](/guestbook/).
6. Please include your site name, URL, avatar or logo URL, a short description, and a way to contact you.


### Maintenance

- dead, inaccessible, or clearly invalid links may be removed without notice.
- Please let me know if your domain, avatar, description, or other information changes.
- If the link to NitreneGlog is removed from your website, I may remove the corresponding link here as well.
- If a link is removed because of a temporary outage, you are welcome to contact me again after the site is restored.
